Round 1 result: SIZEA11 (24,000 characters, text and boxes) and SIZEB05 (24,000 characters, with a picture) both worked.
Round 2 checks:

WHAT IT DECIDES
  - Real labels OK + limit well above 24,000 (e.g. 48,000 or more): convert any StarShipIt PDF to a 300 dpi picture label.
    Simple, works for every layout. A safety limit (setting) refuses any label above the safe size instead of crashing the CMC.
  - Real labels OK but limit close to 24,000: same, but busier labels may not fit -> also build the converter that redraws
    the PDF with normal ZPL commands (smaller, about 3,000 - 6,000).
  - Real labels not readable / not matching: picture approach not good enough -> redraw converter.
